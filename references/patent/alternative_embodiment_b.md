Alternative Embodiment B: 
In an alternative, low-cost embodiment of the invention, several of the advanced features are 
simplified. For example, the Input section (17) is configured for batch classification,where the 
user is instructed to deposit one type of waste at a time, rather than the advanced object detection 
for mixed waste. 
Other automations are also made manual. The automated water sprinkler (not shown) is replaced 
with a manual alert system.The moisture alert system indicates the user about the moisture level 
and they have to sprinkle the water every time. Addition of microbes is also not automated and 
has to be done by the user at the starting of every decomposition batch.The Leftover Food 
Chamber(20) comprises a dual-layer structure including a removable perforated inner tray 
positioned above a garbage bag held within an outer container. Cooked food waste is received on 
the perforated tray, allowing excess liquid to drain through the perforations and collect in a 
leachate tray or cup positioned below the outer container. Optionally, an absorbent pad may be 
placed at the bottom of the bag to retain residual moisture. This configuration ensures that liquids 
are separated from solids without tearing or leaking the garbage bag, while maintaining hygiene 
and odor control. The Inorganic section(19) is just a storage. It stores all the inorganic waste 
collectively instead of a segregated output section(27).
