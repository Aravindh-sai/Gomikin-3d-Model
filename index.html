EDGE-AI COMPUTER VISION & SENSING

Intelligent Segregation at Point of Intake

High-resolution optical classification and multi-sensor telemetry identify and sort waste categories in milliseconds.

👁️

Optical AI Vision Module

Intake chamber camera module with onboard neural inference classifies incoming items into organic, inorganic, or cooked food categories before processing begins.

🧭

Dual-Axis Sorting Base

High-precision 2-axis servo orientation platform tilts to divert validated items into targeted internal compartments with positive mechanical indexing.

📡

Acoustic & Load Telemetry

Integrated ultrasonic distance transducers and structural base load cells continuously compute chamber volumetric capacity and biomass weight.

MECHANICAL COMMINUTION & DRAINAGE

Engineered High-Torque Processing

Rotary shear comminution with continuous hydrostatic moisture extraction accelerates downstream biological digestion.

⚙️

Rotary Cross-Shear Cutters

High-torque drive motor powers balanced rotary shear blades, slicing organic waste down to uniform particle sizes under 8 millimeters.

📐

8 mm Precision Sizing Mesh

Calibrated stainless steel screen regulates particle dimensions, ensuring only optimally sized biomass enters the primary digestion volume.

💧

Hydrostatic Liquid Drainage

Multi-stage drainage channels separate liquid effluent into a sealed base plinth, preventing anaerobic acidification and odors.

THERMOPHILIC BIOMASS CONVERSION

Circular Value Recovery

Aerobic digestion accelerates conversion into stabilized, nutrient-dense biological output ready for soil replenishment.

🌱

Dual-Helix Agitator

Helical mixing blades rotate at controlled low speeds to ensure uniform aeration, moisture distribution, and contact with thermophilic microbes.

🌡️

Closed-Loop Environmental Control

Solid-state temperature and moisture probes regulate thermophilic curing stages, maintaining peak microbiological digestion kinetics.

💨

Negative-Pressure Filtration

Continuous exhaust ventilation draws air through an activated carbon matrix, neutralizing volatile organic compounds and ensuring zero odor escape.
